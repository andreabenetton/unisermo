# LDR-XXXX — Concise decision title

## Context and problem

Describe the observed problem and design context, distinguishing facts from assumptions.

## Alternatives

Compare the viable options, including doing nothing.

## Decision or hypothesis

Record the precise rule or proposition. For experimental records, name it a
hypothesis and avoid describing it as already adopted.

## Evaluation and evidence

State sources, datasets, operational metrics, acceptance/falsification criteria,
uncertainties, and how to reproduce the analysis.

## Consequences and compatibility

Include benefits, risks, dependencies, required migrations, and limitations.

## Decision authority

Who approved this record, when, and on what evidence? If not approved, say so.

## References

Use repo-relative document paths and/or discussion/commit references.
