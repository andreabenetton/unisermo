# May to August 2026: verb and case design sequence

Research audit 24 October 2026. Provenance: C = recovered dated *conversation-index summaries*, **not verbatim transcripts**; G = independently verified Git commits; I = interpretation. A Git import dates a preserved state, not the first invention.

## 24 May: verbal architecture predates the case discussion

- **19:57:28 UTC (C, user)**: design priorities reported as compactness, assonance, and transformed recognizable vocabulary; proposals included verb root `mov`, consonantal past `d`, person vowel and conjunction `et`.
- **19:57:30 UTC (C, assistant)**: example `movdi` and construction `root + tense marker + person vowel`. Early `de` was associated ambiguously with 'of/from'; `ad` and `in` were also considered.
- **20:00:52 UTC (C, user)**: compares `root + tense + person` and `root + person + tense`. Reported argument for the first: person is identifiable at the invariant right edge.
- **21:55:24 UTC (C, user)**: one distinct ending per nominal relation, with basic and extended cases.
- **22:02:25 UTC (C, user)**: genitive/possessive `-s` preferred, reportedly invoking English and Georgian. This is a **user mnemonic**, not a verified claim of identical cross-linguistic morphology.
- **22:05:25 UTC (C, user)**: `-de` assigned exclusively to source/from; rejects prior alternatives `-de` for 'of' and `-da` for 'from'.
- **22:09:05 UTC (C, conversation index)**: historical 7 basic plus 6 extended cases consolidated, as subsequently preserved in the 8 August original language specification.

**Significance:** The earliest firmly indexed 24 May sequence concerns lexical roots and verb layout nearly two hours before the case discussion. The project's inception date is still unknown; no full transcript has yet been archived.

## 10 August: actual synthetic case sequence was 13 → 6 → 7

- **8 August original snapshot:** 13 synthetic cases: seven basic (zero, `-em`, `-s`, `-de`, `-ad`, `-in`, `-ko`) and six extended (`-per -pro -due -fin -tem -mod`).
- **10 August 09:16:52 UTC — [`64c2d33`](https://github.com/andreabenetton/unisermo/commit/64c2d33cb83ded768480f8185582025c380a855e):** reduces to **six** cases: Ø, `-em`, `-s/-es`, `-ad`, `-de`, `-in`. Comitative `-ko` becomes free `kun`; extended suffixes become preposed invariant relators `per pro due fin tem mod`.
- **10 August 14:36:39 UTC — [`53df8ba`](https://github.com/andreabenetton/unisermo/commit/53df8ba04e5d04ef9794c62c357a712567f86123):** adds **seventh** synthetic case `-da` as resultative/transformative predicative, e.g. `Y traes X-em RESULT-da`. This is not a revival of the **rejected May** proposed `-da`='from'. Source `-de` and destination `-ad` remain distinct.

This is an actual *reduction followed by selective expansion*, not an instantaneous 13→7 replacement. Git provides exact sequence and implementation; the full spoken motivations remain to be recovered.

## External phonotactic source check

The August nasal study cites [WALS Maddieson chapter 12](https://wals.info/chapter/12) (61 simple, 274 moderately complex, 151 complex syllable-structure languages, n=486) and [Krämer and Zec 2020](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0952675720000032) (97.9% of restricted word-final coda systems allow nasals; 24.7% allow only nasals). The public publisher pages were checked on 24 October 2026. These support typological priors, **not** human pronunciation or comprehension validation for Unisermo.
