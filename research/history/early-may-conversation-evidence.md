### Interpretation

**First 2026 international project record.** 16 May is the earliest recovered **explicit international-language vowel-design message in the systematic 2026 discussion**. A separate **15 July 2025** user conversation already proposed a tiny and efficient artificial language, but no continuation into the 2026 project is established (see [2025 precursor](2025-minimal-language-precursor.md)). The 6 May Italian/Georgian IPA comparison predates it but does not explicitly claim to be for the artificial language. Calling 6 May its founding date or asserting that the 6 May table *caused* the 22 May alphabet would overstate the record.

**Schwa decision: a genuine reversal.** On 16 May the assistant argues for five robust vowels, with schwa noncontrastive if used at all. The user directly reopens the question on 22 May, accepts a sixth /ə/, and assigns the six categories to six verb persons. That person-morphology need is a **design reason**; no July GlottoDelta data existed yet and none is claimed as the original empirical justification. The 8 August 2026 phonological study will later favor dropping the contrastive sixth vowel, replacing six distinct person vowels with /i u a/ + nasal plurality.

**Georgian contribution: a technical question, not evidence of copying.** The user asks about individual IPA mappings and whether Georgian affricates are one segment; the 22 May invented alphabet uses independent `ts c j` values. The record establishes **chronological adjacency and shared articulatory analysis**, not proof of direct derivation of the invented graphemes from Georgian. It is especially important not to confuse Georgian /d͡z/ with the original alphabet's `j=/dʒ/`.

**Glides and spelling: multiple snapshots, not one frozen choice.** An objection to /w/ and /j/ as too vowel-like preceded a candidate where letter `j` means /dʒ/; `ch` for /tʃ/ was later represented by `c`. By 24 May the full 24-unit original alphabet with `ts,c,j,sh` was preserved. In August, independent affricate status was retired and `j` reassigned to glide /j/. The exact original May timing of adding `ts`, removing `ng` and changing `ch` to `c` needs the full transcript; do not invent intermediate timestamps.

**Retention of soft contrasts.** 24 May already identifies the trade-off between pronunciation accessibility and preserving distinguishable roots: retain f/v and l/r canonically but avoid dangerous lexical minimal pairs. This **predates** the later August protected-minimal-pair model, although full continuity of all surrounding reasoning still needs the full dialogue.
