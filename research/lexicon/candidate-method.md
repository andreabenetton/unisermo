# Candidate-root research method

Under LDR-0008, shortlist only well-defined meanings. Use Kaikki's Latin dictionary for source etymons, and modern-language dictionaries for possible cognates. Actual speaker recognition must be tested, not inferred from shared ancestry. Measure concept frequency in contemporary spoken, written and technical corpora. Favor shorter roots for more frequently communicated concepts, but require collision-free grammar forms, full paradigms and accent-tolerant pronunciations. Compare with existing canonical root and controlled English using real tokenizers. Keep observations and hypotheses distinct. Do not change the confirmed lexicon until independently reviewed and approved via a later LDR.

Initial audit targets: `dik` (dicere), `stru` (struere), `kurar` (cura) and `sentr` (centrum). These are not replacement proposals.
