# Current derivational morphology

> **Status:** canonical where explicitly marked below. Updated 2026-08-09.

## `-ia` — place / institution / field / domain

The suffix **`-ia`** remains canonical. It derives a place, institution, field, or domain associated with the lexical base.

Under the current phonology, adjacent written vowels remain vowels. Therefore **`-ia` is /i.a/**, with hiatus. It is not /ja/; a glide would have to be written explicitly with `j`.

Legacy examples such as `facia` and `docia` are obsolete because the roots `fac` and `doc` were replaced, not because `-ia` ceased to exist. Current derivations must use current lexical roots.

## Related productive suffixes

- `-it` — participial/resultative
- `-itat` — abstract quality/state/property
- `-ia` — place/institution/field/domain, pronounced /i.a/
