# Current question words

> **Status:** partial canonical inventory. Updated 2026-08-09.

The currently confirmed forms relevant to the legacy collision audit are:

- `kom` — HOW / come?
- `ker` — WHY / perche?

Legacy `kur` = WHY is retired. The Latin-derived care/cure family uses `kurar`. The Latin `cum/com-` family uses `kun`, so `kom` remains free for HOW.
