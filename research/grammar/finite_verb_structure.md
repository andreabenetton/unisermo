# Finite Verb Structure

Status: **confirmed**.

## Canonical morphotactics

Every ordinary finite verb has the fixed order:

```text
root + TAM + person vowel + optional N
```

Person vowels are:

- `i` = first person
- `u` = second person
- `a` = third person

The plural morphophoneme `/N/` follows the person vowel when the subject is plural, producing `in`, `un`, `an` in the ordinary orthography.

Examples with `mov-`:

| Structure | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| present | `movi`, `movu`, `mova` | `movin`, `movun`, `movan` |
| past with TAM `d` | `movdi`, `movdu`, `movda` | `movdin`, `movdun`, `movdan` |
| future with TAM `f` | `movfi`, `movfu`, `movfa` | `movfin`, `movfun`, `movfan` |

The ordering rule is morphological, not merely a spelling convention: TAM never follows the person marker, and `/N/` never precedes the person vowel. The inventory and semantics of individual TAM markers remain separately reviewable; this decision fixes their position.
