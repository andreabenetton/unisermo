# Articles and Definiteness

GlottoDelta has **no obligatory articles**. A bare noun is neutral with respect
to definiteness unless context or another determiner supplies the distinction.

## 1. Bare noun

`dom` may correspond contextually to “house”, “a house”, or “the house”.

## 2. Optional indefinite singular `uno`

When explicit indefinite-singular precision is useful, the cardinal numeral
`uno` is reused directly as a determiner:

- `uno dom` — one house / a house
- `uno om` — one person / a person

There is **no separate apocopated article `un`**. The form `un` belongs to the
current second-person plural paradigm.

## 3. Demonstratives

`tik`, `tes`, and `tan` provide deictic identification and may serve where a
more specific referent is intended. They are not pure definite articles.
