## Subsequent tracked changes

- 2026-08-10 — canonical human/person root changed from `om` to `uman`.
  Referential specificity now gives `umano` (male person/man) and `umane`
  (female person/woman); explicit plural is `wi uman`; the regular abstract is
  `umanitat` via `-itat`. All live examples and machine-readable decisions were
  migrated accordingly. The immutable legacy snapshot was not modified.

- 2026-08-10 — finite verb TAM closed as a 3×3 time-aspect system: time `Ø/d/f` × aspect `Ø/n/b`. The inherited synthetic modal `m` was retired; ability, obligation, volition, learned capacity and related servile meanings now use a finite servile verb + bare infinitive.

- 2026-08-10 — participial/resultative `-it` confirmed as directly zero-nominalizable. A nominalized participle follows ordinary noun number: bare `fakit` is number-neutral, `wi fakit` explicitly plural, and `bis fakit` uses the numeral without `wi`. No special participial plural suffix is introduced.
- 2026-08-10 — finite aspect was revised after quantitative phonotactic testing. The consonantal aspect markers `n/b` were retired and replaced by vowel aspect `e/o`, ordered before time: `root + aspect + time + person + N`. Canonical aspect is now `Ø` simple, `e` progressive, `o` completed/perfect; time remains `Ø/d/f`. The nine TAM combinations are `Ø/e/o`, `d/ed/od`, `f/ef/of`. Present aspect-person vowel adjacency is canonical hiatus and may not coalesce if grammatical aspect would be lost.

- 2026-08-10 - nominal case morphology was reduced to a six-case synthetic core: base/nominative zero, accusative `-em`, genitive `-s/-es`, dative/allative `-ad`, ablative `-de`, and locative `-in`. The former comitative `-ko` was retired in favor of analytic `kun + NP`. Former extended case suffixes `-per/-pro/-due/-fin/-tem/-mod` were retired as suffixes and retained as invariant free relators `per/pro/due/fin/tem/mod`. Stale live examples using obsolete nominal plural `-en` were corrected at the same time.

- 2026-08-10 - derivational agent/instrument morphology revised: `-or` is now the canonical person/agent suffix and historical agentive `-er` is retired; `-il` is now the canonical tool/instrument/device suffix and replaces historical instrumental `-or`. Derivational parsing requires a valid canonical base, so comparison words `major` and `minor` remain indivisible function words. The lexical roots `centr` -> `sentr` and `doc` -> `dok` were also confirmed, replacing former `sentrum` and `dots`; `dokor` therefore transparently means teacher/person who teaches.
- 2026-08-10 - canonical state/event phase prefixes were established as fused forms without hyphens: `in-` remain/continue, `ad-` enter/begin, `de-` leave/cease, `re-` return/resume. On `es`: `ines`, `ades`, `dees`, `rees`; on `mov`: `inmov`, `admov`, `demov`, `remov`. `ades` avoids collision with canonical possessive `aes`. These lexical phase operators remain distinct from finite aspect `Ø/e/o`.
- 2026-08-10 - `traes` was canonized as the explicit two-pole state-transition verb with frame `traes DEST-ad SOURCE-de`: destination uses existing dative/allative `-ad`, source uses existing ablative `-de`. `ades X` remains the simpler BECOME construction when no source state is asserted.

- 2026-08-10 - synthetic nominal case was expanded from six to seven values by canonizing resultative/transformative `-da`. Canonical causative-transition frame: `Y-NOM traes X-em RESULT-da`, where `-em` marks the transformed entity and `-da` its resulting state/role/class/property. This remains distinct from `traes DEST-ad SOURCE-de` and ordinary copular predication.
